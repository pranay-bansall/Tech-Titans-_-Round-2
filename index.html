Started from a bedroom in 2023 with 12 restitched jackets.
GENESIS • FROM SHERANWALA GATE

"I make clothes for people who care how they show up."

"Patiala is a city with a royal past. The old maharajas wore the best fabrics, the sharpest turbans and the boldest embroidery in the country. I grew up around that heritage but never saw it in what people my age were wearing. The clothes on offer were either cheap fast fashion or expensive labels that did not feel like us. Nothing was made for a kid from a small city who wanted to look good."

"At 17 I started thrifting jackets, restitching them and selling them to friends from my bedroom. My first drop was 12 pieces and they sold out in a day. My mother thought it was a phase. Honestly, so did I. Then I kept going. I learned pattern cutting from a tailor uncle in Sheranwala Gate and fabrics from a mill owner who took a chance on a teenager with a spreadsheet."

"Today Riyasat is a full label. The name means a princely state, a kingdom. The idea is simple: old royalty, new rules. We take Patiala's heritage and cut it in modern fits, with heavyweight fabrics, clean silhouettes and details made by local artisans. Everything drops in small batches. When it is gone, it is gone."

Fit & Size Guide

We cut clothes intentionally. Understand the four silhouettes before choosing your size.

Oversized

Drop Shoulder • Generous Chest

Designed to hang loose with 4–5 inches of positive ease. Buy your true size for the intended drape, or size down for a standard relaxed fit.

Featured on: Riyasat Heavy Tee, Paranda Hoodie

Boxy

Cropped Length • Wide Shoulders

Hits right at the belt line with square proportions and structured armholes. Gives an elevated, high-fashion stance.

Baggy

Wide Leg • Stacked Ankle

Deep rise with maximum room through the thigh and knee, puddling naturally over chunky sneakers or boots.
